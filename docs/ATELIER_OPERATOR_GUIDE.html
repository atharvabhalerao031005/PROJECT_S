03 / Voice Frequency & The 16-Band Analog Spectrum Visualizer

Understanding the studio audio equalizer and how human speech is isolated from ambient room noise.
Zone A: HVAC Rumble

32Hz to 125Hz bins. Captures air conditioning hum and building vibration. Filtered out so it never falsely triggers voice.

Zone B: Spoken Voice

300Hz to 3400Hz speech formant. Central bars light up caramel and crema when students or instructors speak.

Zone C: Desk Clicks

4kHz to 16kHz high treble. Captures typing, shuffling, and pencil scratches. Filtered out from speech calculations.

FIGURE 3.1: 16-BAND AUDIO SPECTRUM WITH HIGHLIGHTED HUMAN SPEECH FORMANT PASSBAND
Visualizer Mode Toggles

Operators can click the meter pill in the top-right of the visualizer to toggle ballistic physics:

  • VU Ballistic (Recommended): 300ms smoothing inertia; provides a steady, analog meter feel without jitter.
  • Peak Hold: Latches instantaneous volume spikes for 75 frames; ideal for catching sudden questions or loud bursts.
Quick Audio Test Procedure

1. Normal Speech: Speak a sentence toward your mic. Notice Bins 4 to 9 (500Hz to 3.4kHz) immediately surge into warm caramel.

2. Typing Test: Tap your keyboard silently. Treble bins (Zone C) flutter faintly, but speech status remains idle (VAD = false).

Privacy & Compliance FAQ
Q: Are student facial photos or biometric scans stored?

No. Video frames exist strictly as transient pixel matrices in server RAM. They are evaluated for presence and destroyed within 100 milliseconds. No video files, photos, or facial embeds are ever written to disk.

Q: Can Atelier run entirely offline without internet?

Yes. All Haar cascades, Librosa audio filters, and SQLite writes execute locally. If no internet or API key is present, the pedagogical AI uses a 6-state deterministic offline rulebook.
