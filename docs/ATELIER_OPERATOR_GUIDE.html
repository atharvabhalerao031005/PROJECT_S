MATHEMATICAL GATING

Euclidean Centroid Association

When a face bounding box $(x, y, w, h)$ is detected, the engine calculates its central focal coordinate:

cX = x + (w / 2),   cY = y + (h / 2)

The tracker calculates the Euclidean distance between existing tracked participant centroids and new candidate detections. If distance $D ≤ 140\text{px}$, the track identity persists. An occlusion grace period of 3.0 seconds allows brief glances away or coffee sips without dropping the participant.

ATTENDANCE LEDGER COMMITS

The 10-Second Attendance Threshold

Transient hallway passersby or door monitors never trigger accidental attendance logs:

  • Duration < 3.0s: Classified as Transient. Bounding bracket remains subtle roast/mocha.

  • Duration ≥ 10.0s: Verified Present badge unlocks. Bounding brackets glow warm caramel.
  • Automatic SQLite WAL Sync: Writes row to attendance_logs table with first seen, last seen, and duration.

VOICE ACTIVITY DETECTION (VAD)

Librosa Speech Formant Logic

The system isolates spoken classroom dialogue from ambient HVAC noise and shuffling through dual acoustic conditions:

is_speech = (300Hz ≤ Centroid ≤ 3400Hz) && (dBFS > -42.0 dB)

When active, the top badge illuminates: VOICE ACTIVE (-23.4 dB). This signals the AI agent that interactive Socratic debate or student questions are occurring, preventing false inactivity penalties.

METER BALLISTICS MODES

Toggling Ballistic Behavior

Operators can click the meter mode pill in the upper right of the visualizer to adapt response physics:

  • VU Ballistic (Standard): 300ms ANSI integration smoothing; models traditional needle inertia for comfortable visual pacing.
  • Peak Hold: Instantaneous attack with a 75-frame latch; preserves peak energy crests with logarithmic gravity decay.
  • Tape Saturation: Non-linear hyperbolic tangent ($\tanh$) soft compression curve.
PRACTICAL TESTING PROCEDURE
1. Normal Speech Speak a complete sentence into your microphone. Watch the central bands (500Hz to 2.5kHz) light up into caramel and cream foam.

3. Privacy Mute Test Click [Mute Mic]. The entire 16-band ladder drops to idle floor (<0.05). Telemetry badge confirms MIC MUTED.

PRIVACY & COMPLIANCE

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are facial images or biometrics stored?

No. Optical frames are processed exclusively as ephemeral NumPy pixel arrays in volatile RAM. No faces, facial vectors, or video files are ever saved to disk.

Q: Can Atelier run without internet access?

Yes. All Haar cascades, Librosa FFT filters, and SQLite transactions execute locally. The pedagogical AI agent falls back to a deterministic 6-state offline heuristic engine if no API keys are present.
